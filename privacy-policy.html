Privacy Policy

How Maxpro Infotech uses, discloses, and manages user data

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Maxpro Infotech Limited (“Maxpro,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates websites, mobile applications, and business software solutions including Rockey Sales Force Automation, Rocket Sales Pharma, and related products (together, the “Services”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal and business information when you use our Services.

1. Who We Are

Maxpro Infotech Limited is an African ICT company based in Nairobi, Kenya. We provide intelligent, scalable technology for sales, field operations, logistics, inventory, distribution, and related business processes across Africa and beyond.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our Services, we may collect the following categories of data:

2.1 Account and identity information

  • Name, job title, company name, and business contact details
  • Email address, phone number, and login credentials
  • User roles and permissions assigned by your organization

2.2 Business and operational data

  • Customer, outlet, distributor, and partner records entered into our apps
  • Orders, invoices, inventory movements, routes, visits, and sales activity
  • Product catalogues, pricing, promotions, and related commercial data
  • Support tickets, demo requests, and communication history with Maxpro

2.3 Device, location, and usage data

  • Device type, operating system, app version, and unique device identifiers
  • IP address, browser type, and approximate network information
  • App usage logs, crash reports, performance diagnostics, and feature analytics
  • GPS or location data where required for field force, route, or visit verification features (with permission)
  • Camera, gallery, or file access when you capture proof-of-delivery, product images, or upload documents

2.4 Website and marketing data

  • Information submitted through contact, demo, newsletter, or inquiry forms
  • Cookies and similar technologies used to improve site performance and understand traffic

Our Services are primarily designed for business use. Where an organization (our customer) configures accounts for its employees or agents, that organization is typically the data controller for workplace data, and Maxpro processes such data on the customer’s instructions.

3. How We Use User Data

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve our websites, apps, and software platforms
  • Authenticate users, manage access, and secure accounts
  • Enable core product features such as order capture, route planning, inventory visibility, reporting, and field execution
  • Deliver customer support, training, onboarding, and technical assistance
  • Process demo requests, sales inquiries, and service communications
  • Monitor system health, prevent fraud/abuse, and troubleshoot errors
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, contractual, and audit obligations
  • Send service-related notices (for example, downtime, security, or product updates)
  • With appropriate consent or legitimate interest, share relevant product or event information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. How We Disclose User Data

We may disclose information only as needed for the purposes below:

4.1 Within your organization

Data entered into customer-managed workspaces may be visible to authorized administrators and users within that organization according to their configured roles and permissions.

4.2 Service providers

We may share data with trusted processors who help us host infrastructure, send communications, provide analytics, payment facilitation (where applicable), customer support tools, or security monitoring. These providers are required to protect data and use it only for contracted services.

4.3 Business transfers

If Maxpro is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or asset sale, user data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

4.4 Legal and safety disclosures

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or regulator, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of Maxpro, our customers, users, or the public.

4.5 With consent

We may share information for other purposes when you or your organization give us permission to do so.

5. How We Manage and Protect User Data

  • Access controls: Role-based permissions, authentication, and least-privilege access where applicable
  • Security measures: Technical and organizational safeguards such as encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), secure hosting, monitoring, and backup practices
  • Retention: We retain data only as long as needed to provide the Services, meet contractual commitments, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations
  • Customer control: Business customers may manage user accounts, permissions, and certain data lifecycle actions within their tenant/workspace
  • Incident response: We investigate and address suspected security incidents and notify affected parties when required

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We work continuously to strengthen safeguards, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. App Permissions

Depending on the Maxpro app and features enabled by your organization, the application may request device permissions such as:

  • Location — for field visits, route tracking, geo-fencing, or attendance verification
  • Camera / photos / files — for capturing delivery proof, product images, receipts, or documents
  • Network access — to sync data with Maxpro cloud services
  • Notifications — to deliver alerts, reminders, and operational updates
  • Storage — to cache or export operational documents needed offline

You can usually manage these permissions in your device settings. Disabling certain permissions may limit related app functionality.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our websites may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain sessions, measure performance, and improve user experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some site features.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal and business data for as long as an account or contract remains active, plus any additional period required for backups, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or legitimate business records. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it in accordance with our retention practices and customer agreements.

If you are an employee or agent of a Maxpro customer, please contact your organization’s administrator first to request account changes or deletion within that customer’s workspace.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law and your relationship with Maxpro, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion or restriction of processing where legally permitted
  • Object to certain processing, including direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Request a portable copy of certain personal data

To exercise these rights, contact us at helpdesk@maxproinfotech.com. We may need to verify your identity and, where applicable, coordinate with your organization before fulfilling a request.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our Services are intended for business and professional use and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. International Data Transfers

Maxpro serves customers across multiple African countries and beyond. Data may be processed and stored in Kenya or other locations where Maxpro or its service providers operate. Where data is transferred internationally, we take steps designed to provide an appropriate level of protection consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

12. Third-Party Links and Integrations

Our Services may integrate with or link to third-party systems (for example ERP, accounting, payment, messaging, or analytics platforms). Those third parties have their own privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies. Maxpro is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party services outside our control.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when changes are posted. Continued use of the Services after an update means you acknowledge the revised Policy, where permitted by law.
